# Theological Critique: *Disciple Making Lab*

## 1. Executive Verdict

*Disciple Making Lab* (C&MA Central District) is an ecclesiologically pragmatic leadership incubator masquerading as a theology of Christian formation. Its diagnosis of churchgoing consumerism is incisive, and several micro-practices—notably its Lord's Prayer wheel and holistic pedagogy—are genuinely serviceable. Yet beneath evangelical phrasing lies a pervasive **managerial Pelagianism**: a curriculum borrowing its anthropology from Situational Leadership, its ecclesiology from organizational systems theory, and its eschatological confidence from NASA's Apollo program.

By framing discipleship in "process language" divorced from justification, union with Christ, cross-bearing, and sacramental body life, the lab reduces Spirit-wrought regeneration and sanctification to scalable product engineering ("Vehicles," "Betas," "16% tipping points," and "Innovation"). Worse, the Christian and Missionary Alliance's Fourfold Gospel—Christ as Savior, Sanctifier, Healer, and Coming King—is demoted to decorative clip-art that disappears when implementation begins. Sharp as a consulting manual, it requires radical theological surgery before forming Christ in congregations.

## 2. Theological Strengths

Practitioners should preserve five authentic biblical instincts within the curriculum:

1. **Critique of Nominal Church Consumerism:** Diagnosing the passive funnel (`Attend 100% -> Connect 60% -> Serve 30%`, p. 4) and rejecting `"church activity disciples"` (p. 91) rightly confronts cultural Christianity with mutual ministry and multiplication (Eph 4:11–16; 2 Tim 2:2).
2. **Embodied, Anti-Gnostic Pedagogy:** Combining `KNOW / FEEL / DO` (pp. 2, 64) with `"BEING WITH JESUS"` and `"DOING THE THINGS JESUS COULD DO"` (p. 10) resists reducing formation to mere cognitive lecture (Jas 1:22; Mk 3:14).
3. **Subordinating Methods to Christ:** The `Practice -> Tool -> Outcome` progression rightly insists tools serve `"Deeper relationship with Christ"` rather than becoming idols.
4. **Pastoral Care of Leaders:** Pairing skill training with `"SOUL CARE"` (pp. 77–78)—ensuring leaders are personally discipled—counters utilitarian burnout (Acts 20:28).
5. **Liturgical Culture-Making Intuition:** The `Culture Making` wing (`PLAN`: Practices, Language, Artifacts, Narratives) grasps that congregations form people through shared habits, stories, and symbols across generations (Deut 6:6–9).

## 3. Theological Cracks, Blind Spots & Distortions ("Tearing It Apart")

### 3.1 Exegetical Misfires & Gospel Omissions in "Defining a Disciple" (Module 1, p. 8)

Module 1 anchors its `PATH` in a fourfold definition: *"One called by Christ (Rom 8:28–30) / Into a loving relationship with Christ (Mt 22:37) / For the purpose of becoming like Christ (2 Cor 3:18) / While on mission with Christ (2 Cor 5:14–20)."* Every proof-text is exegetically flattened:

- **Rom 8:28–30 ("called by Christ"):** In Paul's golden chain, **God the Father**—not Christ—is the one who foreknows, calls (*ekalesen*), justifies, and glorifies believers *"to be conformed to the image of his Son"* (v. 29). Worse, the formula cites v. 28–30 while silently dropping **justification** and **glorification** from those very verses.
- **Mt 22:37 ("loving relationship with Christ"):** Jesus quotes Deut 6:5, Torah's total covenantal command toward **YHWH**, not therapeutic affection. Omitting v. 39 (*"love your neighbor as yourself"*) severs devotion from neighbor-love.
- **2 Cor 3:18 ("becoming like Christ"):** Paul attributes transformation to **"the Lord, who is the Spirit"** (*apo kyriou pneumatos*) as an unveiled church beholds Christ's glory. Demanding `"process language"` on p. 9 shifts agency from the Spirit's sovereign glory to human technique.
- **2 Cor 5:14–20 ("on mission with Christ"):** Paul's `"ambassadors for Christ"` (v. 20) flows directly from **substitutionary death** (v. 14–15), **new creation** (v. 17), and **imputed righteousness** (v. 19, 21). Leaping to mission skips the gospel indicative creating the missionary.

Five glaring systematic omissions follow:
- **Grace & Justification:** Asking *"How do people, over time, become like Jesus?"* (p. 7) without mentioning sin, repentance, faith, or forensic pardon invites moralistic self-sanctification.
- **Union with Christ:** Prepositions *by, with, like, with* omit **in Christ**—participating in his death and resurrection (Rom 6:1–11; Gal 2:20), the ontological source of holiness.
- **Cross-Bearing & Suffering:** Jesus's explicit criterion—self-denial and taking up one's cross (Lk 9:23; 14:27)—vanishes, despite Rom 8:17–25 and 2 Cor 4:10 framing the cited verses.
- **Sacraments & Body Life:** Baptism appears once as a mere `"Practice"` example (p. 4); the Lord's Supper never appears. The church operates as an organizational pipeline, not the word-and-sacrament Body of Christ.
- **Eschatology & Kingdom Ethics:** Resurrection hope, parousia watchfulness, and Sermon-on-the-Mount ethics (enemy love, justice, truthfulness) are absent.

### 3.2 Managerial Determinism vs. Biblical Pneumatology

The curriculum's master metaphors come from aerospace and systems engineering: `"Moonshot"` hockey-stick growth curves (p. 14), `"16% tipping point"` org theory (p. 4), `"Beta"` launches (pp. 71–72), `"Vehicle Builders"` (pp. 68–70), `"lanyards on Sundays"` (p. 101), and an `"Impact Roadmap"` modeled on NASA's Mercury, Saturn V, and Apollo 4/5/8 missions (p. 99).

This culminates in the prefilled 5+ Year Outcome across Modules 9–10 (pp. 101, 103, 111): *"Our church has made a successful and measurable shift to a disciple making culture **(that could only be derailed if you intentionally tried to stop it)**."*

Theologically, that parenthetical is hubris. Biblical pneumatology portrays Kingdom growth as sovereign, hidden, and organic: the Spirit blows where he wills (Jn 3:8); seed grows while the farmer sleeps without knowing how (Mk 4:26–29); *"neither he who plants nor he who waters is anything, but only God who gives the growth"* (1 Cor 3:7). Promising institutional momentum immune to anything short of deliberate sabotage substitutes **mechanical causality** for **Spirit-dependence**. It erases spiritual drift (Rev 2:4–5), divine pruning (Jn 15:2), and cruciform power perfected in weakness (2 Cor 12:9), while reducing saints to `"Beta"` deliverables measured by `"20%"` vehicle quotas and `"3rd-generation"` output (p. 101).

### 3.3 Decorative Fourfold Gospel Iconography (C&MA Heritage)

The C&MA's Fourfold icons—**Cross** (Savior), **Laver** (Sanctifier), **Pitcher** (Healer), **Crown** (Coming King)—are severed from theological substance:

- **Decorative Cycling (Modules 1–3):** Pages 3–15, 19–27, and 31–37 cycle only those four icons across headers regardless of content (repeating a strict 1-2-3-4 rotation three times in Module 1). `Crown` heads `"Spiritual SWOT"` (p. 5) and `"Moonshot"` (p. 14) with zero eschatology; `Laver` heads `"Character & Competency"` (p. 6) without teaching A. B. Simpson's indwelling Sanctifier; `Pitcher` heads corporate maps (`Master Tool`, `The MAP`) with zero healing theology.
- **Strategic Eviction (Modules 4–10):** Implementation chapters purge the Fourfold symbols for `Compass`, `Car`, `Folded Map`, `Footprints Path`, `Leader Climbing Stairs`, and `Globe`—returning only thrice (`Cross` pp. 73, 113; `Pitcher` p. 108). Even the `Globe` of C&MA world missions is repurposed for internal culture grids. On p. 113's `Cross`-headed Commissioning, disciples are hailed not as heralds of Christ's Kingdom, but **`"YOU ARE CULTURE BUILDERS."`**

## 4. Tool-by-Tool Theological Audit

- **Spiritual SWOT (Module 1, p. 5):** Crossing `Internal/External Control` with `Harmful/Helpful` mislabels managerial triage as "spiritual." Calling `External + Harmful` **`FAILURE`** conflates uncontrollable providence, persecution, or trial (Job; 2 Cor 11) with moral defeat, inducing false guilt. Labeling `External + Helpful/Opportunity` **`BATTLE`** reduces cosmic spiritual warfare (Eph 6:10–18) to opportunistic market capture, while **`BREAKTHROUGH`** (`Internal + Helpful`) locates renewal in self-control.
- **Character & Competency Matrix (Module 1, p. 6):** Rightly warns against skilled leaders lacking integrity (1 Tim 3; Ps 78:72). Yet promising high-character, high-competency leaders **`"Unlimited potential for kingdom breakthrough"`**—while godly low-competency believers possess `"Limited potential"`—is triumphalist Pelagianism. God routinely works decisive kingdom victory through weak, unimpressive vessels so no flesh boasts (1 Cor 1:26–29; 2 Cor 4:7).
- **The MAP / Apprenticeship Square (Module 1, p. 13):** Mapping sanctification onto Situational Leadership skill stages (`Unconscious Incompetence` to `Unconscious Competence`) distorts spiritual maturity. Mature believers never graduate into autonomous `"Unconscious Competence"` (`"You do, I cheer"`); sanctification deepens conscious poverty of spirit and reliance on Christ (Jn 15:5; 1 Tim 1:15). Calling D3's temptation **`"Co-dependence"`** pathologizes biblical mutual bearing of burdens (Gal 6:2), and co-opting Psalm 23's **`"Valley of the shadow"`** for D2 skill frustration (`"I quit"`) trivializes mortal suffering.
- **Bible Reading Tool (Module 1, p. 11; Module 3, p. 31):** Commendably simple and obedience-oriented (`Chew -> Digest -> Act`). However, moving straight from `"What does the text say?"` to **`"What is the Holy Spirit stirring in me?"`** and a takeaway **`"Word or Phrase"`**—without historical context, law-gospel clarity, Christocentric reading (Lk 24:27), or ecclesial discernment (2 Pet 1:20)—invites atomistic subjectivism, explaining the loose proof-texting on p. 8.
- **Jesus Pattern Tool (Module 1, p. 12; Module 3, p. 35):** The `UP / IN / OUT` triangle helpfully integrates communion, community, and mission. Yet flattening Jesus's unrepeatable *messianic* vocation into a balanced human rhythm blurs *exemplum* and *sacramentum*: Christ's "UP" was eternal filial obedience toward Gethsemane, and his "OUT" was atoning ransom (Mk 10:45). Moreover, the snow-capped mountain lacks **"DOWN"**—kenotic descent and cross-bearing (Phil 2:5–8).
- **Prayer Tool Wheel (Module 3, p. 33):** The curriculum's finest tool. Patterned on the Lord's Prayer (`FATHER`; `Character`, `Kingdom`, `Provision`, `Forgiveness`, `Guidance`, `Deliverance`) and anchored as `Worship-based, Scripture-fed, Spirit-led`, it teaches Trinitarian, Scripture-saturated prayer. Still, reducing Mt 6:12 to one-word `"Forgiveness"` drops its horizontal reconciliation mandate (*"as we also have forgiven"*), while Christ's priestly mediation (Heb 4:14–16) and biblical lament remain unnamed.
- **Training Triangle (Module 2, p. 23):** Pairing `Information` with `Imitation` restores Pauline life-on-life apprenticeship (1 Cor 11:1; 1 Thess 2:8). Making the third vertex **`"Innovation (take what you've received and run with it)"`**, however, replaces 2 Tim 2:2's mandate to guard and entrust apostolic truth with Silicon Valley disruption—encouraging doctrinal drift as churches invent five or six custom tools (p. 61).
- **Abrupt Prayer of Examen (Module 6, p. 73):** Inserted without historical framing or any link to Module 3's Prayer Wheel, this morning adaptation strips classic Ignatian Examen of evening conscience-searching, sin-confession, and repentance. Asserting *"just your desire for a moment with God indicates the presence of God within you"* conflates natural longing with saving indwelling, while grounding `"identity in God"` purely in creation (*"as God made you... gifts and talents"*) omits Christ, regeneration, and forgiveness entirely—ironically printed beneath a Cross icon.

## 5. Theological Reconstruction Recommendations

1. **Re-Anchor "Defining a Disciple" in the Gospel:** Replace p. 8's skewed proof-texts with an explicitly Trinitarian, cruciform definition grounded in **justification by faith** (Rom 5:1; Eph 2:8–10), **union with Christ** (Gal 2:20), **Spirit-wrought sanctification** (2 Cor 3:18), **cross-bearing** (Lk 9:23), and **Word-and-sacrament ecclesial life** (Acts 2:42; 1 Cor 12:12–27).
2. **Dethrone Engineering Determinism:** Excise the NASA Apollo roadmap (p. 99), `"Moonshot"` inevitability (p. 14), and the reckless guarantee across pp. 101–111 (`"only be derailed if you intentionally tried to stop it"`). Ground planning in agricultural dependence (1 Cor 3:6–9; Mk 4:26–29) that anticipates suffering, pruning, and divine sovereignty.
3. **Install Hermeneutical & Apostolic Guardrails in Tools:** Add *"What did this mean in context / How does it reveal Christ?"* before Band 2 of the Bible Tool; replace `"Innovation"` with **"Faithful Multiplication"** (2 Tim 2:2); revise `The MAP` away from `"Unconscious Competence"` toward growing dependence; and rename `FAILURE`/`BATTLE` on the Spiritual SWOT.
4. **Integrate Prayer Around the Wheel and Cross:** Either remove Module 6's detached Examen or rewrite it around Christ's atonement and the `Prayer Tool Wheel`'s `Forgiveness` and `FATHER` framework.
5. **Restore the Fourfold Gospel Substantively:** Stop rotating header clip-art and build actual modules showing how Christ as **Savior**, **Sanctifier**, **Healer**, and **Coming King** animates every practice—re-commissioning `"Culture Builders"` (p. 113) as witnesses of the crucified and coming Lord.
