# Instructional Design & Learning Experience Critique: *Disciple Making Lab*

## 1. Executive Verdict

Conceptually, **Disciple Making Lab** (10 modules, 113 workbook pages) rests on an admirable systems insight: sustainable church transformation requires aligning cultural architecture (**Culture Making / PLAN**) with relational multiplication (**Relational Apprenticeship / PATH**). However, strictly as an instructional product and learner experience, the printed workbook suffers from severe **instructional-design neglect, structural drift, and extraneous cognitive load**.

While a skilled live facilitator can temporarily mask these flaws in the room, the printed curriculum fails as a scaffolded learning instrument and post-session job aid. Over a quarter of the workbook (**30 of 113 pages, 26.5%**) is purely blank or notes-only—rising past **40%** when counting bare heading-plus-`NOTES:` sheets. Core models appear unlabeled, key exercises duplicate across months without progression, Modules 4–5 push novice learners over a steep Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) cliff without rubrics, and visual iconography fractures across Modules 4–10. The curriculum teaches teams to build *"simple, back-of-a-napkin, transferable"* tools while delivering an artifact that is neither self-explanatory nor cleanly sequenced.

---

## 2. Pedagogical Strengths

Despite major execution gaps in the workbook, four genuine pedagogical strengths anchor the course design:

1. **Coherent Dual-Wing Macro-Architecture**: The **Discipling Culture Master Tool** (Bowtie diagram, pp. 4, 19, 74) effectively bridges organizational systems (`Attend 100% -> Connect 60% -> Serve 30%`, `16%` tipping point via `PLAN`) and peer-to-peer multiplication (`2%` through `Community / Family / City` into `PATH`). Anchoring a multi-month cohort in one visual mental model mitigates the siloed thinking typical of church leadership training.
2. **Authentic Experiential & Cohort Transfer Loops**: Rather than front-loading passive lecture, Modules 1–9 apply adult learning principles (andragogy) through immediate workplace application: interviewing parishioners on disciple definitions (M1–2, pp. 15, 21, 27), field-testing one tool with a real person using four sharp diagnostic questions (`What worked?`, `What didn't?`, `Missing/confusing?`, `Would you use/teach it?`, M3 p. 37), and launching a 3-month Beta group with defined success indicators (M6 p. 71).
3. **Pockets of Exemplary Micro-Scaffolding**: Two sequences demonstrate high-craft instructional design:
   - **Module 6's Vehicle Builder (pp. 68–71)** converts abstract design into concrete constraints: nine group-design questions (p. 68), a timed three-part meeting template (`Celebrate 10 min -> Discipleship Focus -> Next Step 10 min`, p. 69), two worked weekly rhythm examples (p. 70), and a seven-point Beta Launch Checklist (p. 71).
   - **Module 9's Impact Roadmap (pp. 97–103)** leverages dual-coding—pairing a concrete visual horizon metaphor (`Foreground 90 days` to `Beyond-the-Horizon 5–20 years`, p. 97) with a non-threatening Apollo worked example (p. 99) before transitioning to a church-specific example (p. 101).
4. **Whole-Person Formation Integration**: Opening Module 1 with autobiographical reflection (p. 3), pairing tactical Beta planning with the `Prayer of Examen` (M6 p. 73), and closing with `Soul Care` (M6 pp. 77–78) and communal commissioning (M10 pp. 112–113) honor both cognitive and spiritual formation.

## 3. Structural Flaws, Cognitive Load & Redundancy Audit ("Tearing It Apart")

### Redundancy, Sequencing Drift & Orphaned Assignments
* **Five-Fold Culture-Making Grid Inflation & p. 108 Orphan**: The 4x5 `culture_making_grid` (`PLAN` x ministry contexts) appears **five times** across four consecutive modules: **M6 p. 75** (*Examples*), **M6 p. 76** (*Ideas*), **M7 p. 84** (*Creation*), **M8 p. 90** (*What Could Be*), and **M9 p. 108** (*Review*). Pages 84 and 90 print the *exact same* prompt (*"List specific ways you can intentionally use each culture-making element to spark a God-moment..."*), forcing teams to re-invent or transcribe the same 20-cell matrix repeatedly while column labels inconsistently drift (`Service/Groups/Kids/Youth/Other` on pp. 75, 76, 90 vs. `Worship Service/Groups/Children/Students/Other` on pp. 84, 108). Worse, **p. 108 is a textbook production error**: placed *after* Module 9's `NEXT STEPS` (p. 107), omitted from p. 107's checklist, stamped with a stray M1–3 `Pitcher` icon instead of `Globe`, and **never debriefed in Module 10** (pp. 109–113), leaving its reflection prompt (*"BRING BACK 1-2 PRACTICAL QUESTIONS"*) completely orphaned.
* **Quickfire Language Duplication & Inverted Sequence**: Module 7 (p. 85) asks each participant to write three repeatable macro-culture sentences; Module 8 (p. 88) re-prints the identical `QUICKFIRE LANGUAGE EXERCISE` title and *"Write your three sentences/statements below"*, even though M7's `NEXT STEPS` (p. 86) never assigned homework on it. Sequentially, M8 forces teams to wordsmith macro-language *before* auditing current reality (`PLAN INVENTORY: "WHAT IS"`, p. 89) or selecting priorities (`"WHAT COULD BE?"`, pp. 90–91)—and isolates language creation from both the `Language` row of the Grid (p. 84) and the `Communication Folio` (pp. 92–93).
* **Static Master Tool Repetition**: `discipling_culture_master_tool` repeats unaltered on pp. 4, 19, and 74—even mislabeled `CONTENT INPUT: CULTURE-MAKING REVIEW` on p. 74 despite printing the full Bowtie. Yet it vanishes during Module 5 (`PATH` Inventory, p. 51) and Modules 9–10 (`PATH`/`PLAN` Impact Roadmap), behaving like decorative wallpaper rather than a cumulative progression map.
* **Bible Reading & Jesus Pattern Pedagogical Regression**: Module 1 trains both tools with active scaffolding (`MODEL` and `YOU TRY IT`, pp. 11–12), then Module 3 re-trains the exact same tools (pp. 31, 35) alongside the `Prayer Tool` (p. 33) while **stripping out `MODEL / YOU TRY IT`** in favor of passive `NOTES:`. With zero tool practice assigned in M1's `NEXT STEPS` (p. 15) and zero tool coverage in M2, Module 1 suffers acute intrinsic overload (packing **7 major frameworks** into 1.5 days, including `Spiritual SWOT` and `Character & Competency` which are never explicitly revisited) only for Module 3 to regress into passive note-taking.
* **Vanishing `KNOW/FEEL/DO` Framing**: `KNOW/FEEL/DO NOTES` appears solely on the two multi-day retreat schedule pages (M1 p. 2, M6 p. 64)—placed *ahead* of instruction with zero usage prompts—and disappears entirely in Modules 2–5 and 7–10. It reflects template carryover from retreat flyers rather than a deliberate cognitive-affective-behavioral synthesis loop.
* **Broken Beta Loops & Late-Course Editorial Decay**: Modules 7, 8, and 9 `NEXT STEPS` (pp. 86, 94, 107) each explicitly promise Beta debriefs in the following session—yet **Modules 8, 9, and 10 contain zero printed Beta debrief pages** (only M7 p. 82 has a bare `NOTES:` page). Simultaneously, Module 2's `Training Triangle` (p. 23) is taught months before tools or vehicles exist and is reduced to an unlabeled micro-icon in M6 (`Onboarding Training`, p. 77); Module 9's ministry worked example (p. 101) leaves `1-Year Objective` completely blank (`""`); and `modulePage` numbering collapses to `null` across all content pages in Modules 7–10.

### Cognitive Load & "Blank-Workbook Syndrome"
Modules 2–5 devote **exactly 50% of every page** to blank/notes-only spreads (6/12, 5/10, 5/10, and 7/14 pages; plus 6/14 in M9). Combined with sparse `NOTES:`-only printed sheets (pp. 41, 53, 55, 57, 59, 65–67, 72, 79, 82, 89, 92–93, 105, 110), learners spend immense working memory transcribing baseline facilitator definitions instead of processing contextual strategy:
- **Split-Attention & Transience Effects**: Nine canonical models omit essential printed legends: `Spiritual SWOT` (p. 5) omits both axes (`Internal/External`, `Harmful/Helpful`); `Character & Competency` (p. 6) omits quadrant outcomes; `Discipling Culture Master Tool` (pp. 4, 19, 74) omits funnel percentages (`100%/60%/30%`), `16%`/`2%` metrics, `PLAN` words, and `PATH` words; `Bible Reading Tool` (pp. 11, 31) omits `Holy Spirit` and `Chew/Digest/Act`; `Jesus Pattern` (pp. 12, 35) omits `UP/IN/OUT`; `The MAP` (p. 13) omits all 4 stages (`D1/L1`–`D4/L4`), leadership/disciple postures, and temptations (`"I quit"`, `Co-dependence`); `Moonshot Curve` (p. 14) omits axes; `Training Triangle` (p. 23) omits `Information/Imitation/Innovation`; `Prayer Wheel` (p. 33) omits all 6 segments and 3 pillars; and `Picture/Mirror/Window` (p. 43) omits labels and definitions.
- **Zero Standalone Reference Integrity**: Any pastor missing a session—or attempting to onboard church elders back home—holds an unreadable booklet of blank shapes.

## 4. Workbook UX, Scaffolding, Assessment & Diagram Usability Critique

### Scaffolding & ZPD Mismatch (Modules 4–5)
Asking church leaders to become original visual-pedagogy designers in Modules 4–5 drastically exceeds their Zone of Proximal Development:
1. **Unbridged Exposure-to-Creation Cliff**: After encountering only 3 pre-built tools in Module 3 (without printed visual-design rationales), teams sit through two unprinted input sessions (pp. 41, 43) and six generic workshop steps (p. 45), then face **four nearly empty workshop pages** (`TOOL #2`–`TOOL #5`, pp. 53, 55, 57, 59) with instructions to return to Module 6 holding **5–6 completed, original tools** (p. 61). There are no worked tool-creation examples (e.g., transforming a messy draft into a napkin visual), no printed `Practice -> Tool -> Outcome` worksheets, and no link back to Module 1's `10 Practices` (p. 10).
2. **Absence of Quality Rubrics & Calibration**: Both M4 (p. 47) and M5 (p. 61) instruct: *"MODIFY YOUR TOOL UNTIL YOU THINK IT IS 90% DONE."* Relying on *"you think"* without a rubric directly induces the `D1` (*Unconscious Incompetence*) blind spot warned against by the course's own `MAP` model (p. 13). Ironically, `coreFrameworks.toolPhilosophy` contains five strong criteria (`Simple/napkin`, `Visual`, `Process-oriented`, `Transferable`, `101-level`) and three lenses (`Picture, Mirror, Window`), yet **none are printed as a self-check rubric** or peer-scorecard during `TOOLKIT PRESENTATIONS` (pp. 65–66).
3. **Eroding Feedback Protocols**: Module 3's four diagnostic testing prompts (p. 37) vanish in Modules 4–5, replaced by generic *"SHARE YOUR TOOL WITH ONE PERSON"* debriefs (pp. 47, 61) with no criteria for tester selection (e.g., non-staff layperson vs. fellow pastor) and no facilitator approval gate comparable to M2's emailed definition review (p. 27). Similarly, `COMMUNICATION FOLIO` (pp. 92–93) offers headers (`ONE-PAGER`, `STAKEHOLDERS & TIMELINE`) with zero scaffolded fields.

### Diagram Usability & Iconography Audit (16 Diagrams + Page Icons)
Evaluating the 16 printed diagrams as visual instruments exposes stark inconsistency:
* **High-Usability Benchmarks**: `vehicle_builder_meeting_design` (p. 69) combines sequential numbers (`#1`–`#3`), semantic icons (party popper, magnifying glass, footprints), and actionable prompts. `impact_roadmap_introduction` (p. 97) and `impact_roadmap_template` (pp. 99, 101, 103, 111) map spatial distance cleanly onto planning horizons (though collapsing `1-Year Objective` into a single box breaks the `PATH`/`PLAN` column split visible in 3-Year and 90-Day tiers).
* **Moderate Friction**: `character_competency_matrix` (p. 6) labels axes clearly but omits quadrant stakes; `prayer_tool_wheel` (p. 33) establishes clockwise sequence and `FATHER` anchor but blanks out all six sectors; `culture_making_grid` suffers only from label drift and over-repetition.
* **Severe Visual Affordance Failures**:
  - `the_map_apprenticeship_square` (p. 13) stacks four nouns (`CONFIDENCE, COMPETENCE, ENTHUSIASM, EXPERIENCE`) inside a box with zero corner labels or directional cycle cues—plus creates a **lexical collision** (`The MAP` square vs. `PATH`'s `Map` component, whose icon is a folded map).
  - `jesus_pattern_tool` (pp. 12, 35) vs. `training_triangle` (p. 23) vs. `leader_development_overview` (p. 77) create a **three-way triangle shape collision**, while `bible_reading_tool` (pp. 11, 31) offers three visually indistinguishable horizontal bands and an uncaptioned downward arrow.
  - `spiritual_swot_matrix` (p. 5) creates schema interference (non-SWOT labels on a SWOT-named chart without axis anchors); `moonshot_exercise_curve` (p. 14) places `STOP` ambiguously at the peak of an accelerating curve; `whats_your_path_inventory` (p. 51) rotates labels vertically (degrading scan speed) and stacks horizontal journey steps vertically; and `tool_metaphors_picture_mirror_window` (p. 43) prints three uncaptioned line drawings.
* **Broken Page-Level Icon Grammar**: Modules 1–3 rotate four C&MA denominational symbols (`Cross`, `Pitcher`, `Crown`, `Chalice`) arbitrarily across unrelated exercises (e.g., `Bible Tool` is `Chalice` in M1 and `Pitcher` in M3). Modules 4–10 pivot to functional icons (`Compass`, `Folded Map`, `Car`, `Globe`, `Footprints Path`, `Lightbulb`), yet immediately contradict them:
  - **Compass (`Tools`)** is used for `TOOLKIT PRESENTATIONS` (pp. 65–66) instead of a presentation/feedback icon, blurring tool design with peer evaluation.
  - **Footprints Path** (visually echoing `#3. NEXT STEP` on p. 69) is misapplied across unrelated Culture-Making and reflection pages: `PLAYBOOK FOR THE CHURCH` (p. 67), `QUICKFIRE LANGUAGE EXERCISE` (pp. 85, 88), `COMMUNICATION FOLIO` (pp. 92–93), `COACHING & QUESTIONS` (p. 105), and `SHARING THE LEARNING` (p. 112).
  - **Coaching pages use three different icons**: `Lightbulb` (p. 79), `Footprints Path` (p. 105), and `Globe` (p. 110).
  - **Folded Map** collides between `Map` inside `PATH` (pp. 4, 51) and `NEXT STEPS` action pages (M4–9), while **Pitcher** resurfaces inexplicably on p. 108.

## 5. Instructional Redesign Recommendations

1. **Eliminate Blank-Workbook Syndrome with Progressive Dual-Coding**: Replace empty `NOTES:` verso spreads and bare shapes with printed canonical axes, vertex/band/segment headers, and concise 3–5 word definitions (or perforated Facilitator Reference Cards). Retain structured fill-in prompts and dedicated contextual application boxes so working memory focuses on *church adaptation* rather than verbatim transcription.
2. **Streamline Sequence, Cut Redundancy & Restore Closing Synthesis**:
   - Remove `Bible` and `Jesus Pattern` training from Module 1 (reducing M1 to Master Tool, Destination, Character/Competency -> 10 Practices, and MAP) and restore `MODEL / YOU TRY IT` active practice in Module 3.
   - Collapse the five Culture-Making Grids into one fold-out **Master Culture-Making Canvas** updated across M6–8 (deleting p. 108 entirely).
   - Move `Quickfire Language` to a single M8 slot *after* `PLAN Inventory` (`"WHAT IS"`), feeding directly into a templated `Communication Folio` (stakeholder/channel matrix).
   - Add structured Beta Debrief scorecards to Modules 8–10, populate M9's `1-Year Objective` example (p. 101), and move `KNOW/FEEL/DO` synthesis prompts to the **final page of all 10 modules**.
3. **Scaffold Modules 4–5 Within Learners' ZPD**: Print `Practice -> Tool -> Outcome`, `Picture/Mirror/Window`, and a **5-Point Tool Quality Rubric** (`Simple/Napkin, Visual, Process-Oriented, Transferable, 101-Level`) directly onto pp. 41–47 and 65–66. Require **2–3 high-craft tools** mapped explicitly to M1's `10 Practices` (replacing blank `TOOL #2–#5` pages with structured 6-step design canvases and a worked "bad draft -> good napkin tool" exemplar), and extend M3's 4-question field-test rubric into M4–5 with layperson testing requirements.
4. **Standardize Visual Affordances & Icon Taxonomy**: Label every diagram's geometry directly, untangle `"The MAP"` vs. `"Map"` and the three triangles via distinct visual cues, align `whats_your_path_inventory` (p. 51) horizontally with legible labels, restore `modulePage` pagination in M7–10, and enforce strict 1-to-1 corner iconography (`Globe` = Culture-Making/Language/Comms, `Compass` = Tool Design, `Speech/Checklist` = Coaching/Presentations, `Checkmark` = Next Steps).
